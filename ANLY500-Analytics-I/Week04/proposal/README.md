Provide a written project proposal that meets the requirements in the final project guidelines and uses the formatting described below.

### Purpose of the proposal

The project proposal is intended to introduce the company and its situation, problem, or challenge. It should include all relevant background information needed to understand the context of your planned analysis.

Your proposal should address the following questions:

- What is the problem you are trying to solve or the question you are trying to answer?
- What data do you need (or already have)?
- What work do you plan to do in the project (e.g., cleaning, exploration, modeling, visualization)?
- Which algorithms/techniques/models do you plan to use or develop? Be as specific as you can.
- How will you evaluate what you’ve done (metrics, validation approach, business impact, etc.)?
- What do you expect to submit or accomplish by the end of the project?

### Formatting and submission requirements

- Length: 2 pages (1 page of cover page, **2 pages of your writing**, "abstract" is not required in your proposal, and 1 page is your reference)
- Font: 12 pt Times New Roman
- File type: Word (.docx) or PDF
- Line spacing: double-spaced
- Style: APA formatting

Submit your completed proposal following the instructions provided in your course LMS.
