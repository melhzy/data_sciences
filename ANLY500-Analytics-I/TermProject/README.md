# Term Project Requirements

**Course:** ANLY 500 - Analytics I  
**Type:** Individual or Team-Based  

## Deliverables & Grading

| Deliverable | Weight | Due Date |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Project Proposal** | 20% | See Canvas |
| **2. Presentation** | 40% | See Canvas |
| **3. Final Report** | 40% | See Canvas |

---

## 1. Project Proposal
The proposal introduces the company, situation, and the problem/challenge to be addressed.

*   **Length:** 1-2 pages
*   **Format:**
    *   12 pt font, Times New Roman
    *   Double Spacing
    *   APA Formatting
    *   Word or PDF
*   **Key Questions to Answer:**
    1.  What is the problem/question?
    2.  What data do you need?
    3.  What is the plan?
    4.  Which algorithms/models will be used?
    5.  How will you evaluate success?

## 2. Presentation
A comprehensive overview of the project, serving as a draft for the final report.

*   **Length:** 10 slides minimum
*   **Format:** PowerPoint (.pptx), APA Formatting
*   **Content Requirements:**
    *   Problem description & background literature
    *   Data source, description, and EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis)
    *   Methodology (tools, algorithms, techniques)
    *   Results (or preliminary test results)
    *   Challenges faced & remaining work

## 3. Final Report
The complete documentation of the project.

*   **Length/Format:** Refer to `ANLY_500_Report_Formatting` and `final_project_guidelines.docx`.
*   **Structure:**
    1.  **Introduction:** Motivation, problem statement, preliminary insights.
    2.  **Related Work:** Background on the company/problem, similar studies.
    3.  **Data:** Complete description, sources, cleaning, and EDA.
    4.  **Technical Approach:** Detailed process, algorithms applied, and methodology.
    5.  **Test & Evaluation:** Validation methods, results analysis, baseline comparison, and reproducibility.
    6.  **Conclusion:** Summary of findings and future work.

---
*Note: For individual projects, all deliverables are submitted by the single author. "Team contribution" scores will be based on individual performance.*
