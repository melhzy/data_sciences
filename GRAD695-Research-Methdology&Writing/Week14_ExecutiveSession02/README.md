### Presentation Requirements

#### Content (5 minutes)
1. **Research Problem** (1 min)
   - Clear problem statement
   - Research significance and motivation

2. **Research Questions** (30 sec)
   - 2-4 specific research questions
   - Expected contributions

3. **Methods** (2 min)
   - Research design overview
   - Data sources and collection approach
   - Analytical methods and models
   - Validation strategy

4. **Expected Outcomes** (1 min)
   - Anticipated findings or deliverables
   - Theoretical and practical contributions

5. **Q&A Preparation** (30 sec)
   - Be prepared to answer questions about methodology, data, and feasibility

#### Presentation Materials
- **Slides:** Professional PowerPoint or Google Slides
- **Recommended:** 5-7 slides maximum
- **Visual aids:** Key figures, diagrams, or model architecture
- **Time management:** Practice to stay within 5 minutes

#### Evaluation Criteria
- **Clarity:** Clear communication of research problem and approach
- **Organization:** Logical flow and structure
- **Content:** Appropriate depth and detail for 5 minutes
- **Delivery:** Professional presentation skills
- **Visuals:** Effective use of slides and graphics
- **Time management:** Stays within 5-minute limit
- **Q&A:** Ability to respond to questions effectively

## Presentation Tips

### Before the Session
- **Practice multiple times** - stay within 5 minutes
- **Prepare for questions** - anticipate methodology and feasibility questions
- **Test technology** - ensure slides display correctly
- **Arrive early** - set up and test equipment
- **Print backup** - bring handout of slides in case of technical issues

### During Presentation
- **Speak clearly** - project your voice
- **Make eye contact** - engage with audience
- **Explain jargon** - define technical terms briefly
- **Show enthusiasm** - convey passion for your research
- **Watch time** - glance at clock/timer

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Going over 5 minutes
- ❌ Too many slides (causes rushing)
- ❌ Reading directly from slides
- ❌ Too much technical detail
- ❌ Unclear research questions
- ❌ Vague methodology
- ❌ No visual aids or all text slides
