## Weekly Topic

Creating effective research presentations and visualizations.

### Constructing Effective Visuals (In-Class Exercise)
- **Points:** 20 points
- **Description:** Create and critique research visualizations

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:
1. Design effective research presentations
2. Create clear and informative visualizations
3. Tell compelling research stories
4. Use visual design principles
5. Present complex information simply

## Key Concepts

- Visualization best practices
- Color theory and accessibility
- Figure design for publications
- Presentation structure
- Storytelling with data
- Avoiding common visualization pitfalls
- Conference vs. stakeholder presentations

## Key Deliverables

- Understanding of effective visualization techniques
- Practice creating research figures
- Preparation for final presentation
