## Course Description

This course guides students to develop and finalize a selected research problem and construct a proposal that effectively establishes the basis for launching an experiential capstone project. The course provides an overview of strategies for effective problem investigation and solution proposal. Research methodology is studied and applied as part of suggesting a solution to a problem. Writing and formatting techniques are explored and applied as a communication tool for cataloging the investigation and recommending the solution.

## Learning Objectives

- Select and refine a research topic
- Locate and analyze relevant literature
- Formulate specific research questions that address practical or conceptual problems
- Craft a research plan with appropriate design, methods, and analyses
- Master key elements of scientific writing
- Combine these elements in a coherent, concise proposal

## Final Proposal Requirements

The final proposal should be:
- Double spaced, 12-pt font, 1-inch margins
- Include: Title page, Introduction (1-2 pages), Related Works/Literature Review (3-5 pages), Methods Section (2-4 pages), Bibliography/References (20-30 citations)
