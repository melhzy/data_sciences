**Date:** June 1-5, 2026

## Weekly Topic

Effective research writing, revision strategies, and professional presentation of research proposals.

### Translating Technical Concepts (In-Class Exercise)
- **Points:** 20 points
- **Description:** Practice explaining technical concepts for different audiences

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:
1. Write clear, concise research prose
2. Revise and edit effectively
3. Explain technical concepts to diverse audiences
4. Follow academic writing conventions
5. Prepare polished research documents

## Key Concepts

- Academic writing style
- Active vs. passive voice
- Clarity and conciseness
- Paragraph structure
- Transitions and flow
- Audience awareness
- Revision strategies
- Common writing errors

## Key Deliverables

- Understanding of effective research writing
- Practice translating technical concepts
- Preparation for proposal revision
