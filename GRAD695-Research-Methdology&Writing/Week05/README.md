**Date:** April 13-17, 2026

## Weekly Topic

Full scope of the research proposal — what each section must accomplish and how they connect into one coherent argument. Covers the Introduction, Literature Review, Purpose Statements & Research Questions, Methods/Research Design, and Feasibility.

## Tutorial

📄 [View Tutorial (HTML)](https://melhzy.github.io/data_sciences/GRAD695-Research-Methdology&Writing/Week05/05_research_basics.html)

### Topics Covered

**Part 1 — The Introduction**
- Four components: Narrative Hook, Background, Importance, Goal Statement
- Goal statement templates and verb selection (explore, examine, determine, build)
- Common mistakes in structuring introductions

**Part 2 — The Literature Review**
- Broad-to-specific funnel structure
- How to read your first few papers (five-step routine)
- What a literature review IS vs. IS NOT
- Citation management with Zotero (workflow, setup, weekly pacing)
- Ending the literature review (methodological approach + purpose + RQ)

**Part 3 — Purpose Statements & Research Questions**
- Purpose statement types by research design (describe, explore, explain, predict)
- Hypothesis testing: null vs. alternative, p-values vs. effect size
- Research questions: characteristics, weak vs. strong examples
- When to use hypotheses vs. research questions

**Part 4 — Research Design (Methods Section)**
- Three building blocks: Data, Measurement, Analysis
- Data quality dimensions (accuracy, label validity, real-world fit)
- Sampling and population vs. sample
- Types of variables (categorical, numeric, engineered features)
- Conceptual vs. operational definitions
- Matching analytic plan to project goal

**Part 5 — Feasibility**
- Five feasibility questions (data access, scope/timeline, skills/tools, ethics/approvals, contingency)
- 12-week timeline budget for a one-semester capstone
- Scope-reduction strategies
- Green/red self-assessment grid
- Writing contingency plans
