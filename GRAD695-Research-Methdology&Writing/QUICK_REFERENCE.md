## Final Proposal Requirements

### Length & Format
- 6-11 pages of content (plus title page & references)
- Double-spaced, 12-pt font, 1-inch margins
- APA 7th edition style
- 20-30 citations

### Required Sections
1. **Title Page**
2. **Introduction** (1-2 pages)
   - Background, problem statement, research questions, objectives, significance
3. **Literature Review** (3-5 pages)
   - Theoretical frameworks, prior research, gaps in literature
4. **Methods** (2-4 pages)
   - Research design, data collection, analysis plan, validation strategy
5. **References** (APA format)

## Tips for Success

### Time Management
- **Start early** on major assignments (multiple drafts recommended)
- **Schedule consultations** as soon as slots open
- **Use feedback** from each assignment to improve the next
- **Block time** every week for proposal work

### Quality Proposal Writing
- Read examples of good proposals in your field
- Get feedback early and often
- Revise multiple times
- Have someone proofread your writing
- Follow the template structure
- Cite as you write (don't wait until the end)

### Research Process
1. **Weeks 1-4:** Topic selection, literature review
2. **Weeks 5-7:** Data planning, methodology design
3. **Weeks 8-10:** Detailed methods, validation planning
4. **Weeks 11-13:** Writing, revision, presentation prep
5. **Weeks 14-15:** Final polish and submission

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Waiting until the last week to write proposal
- ❌ Ignoring feedback from instructor
- ❌ Choosing a topic that's too broad
- ❌ Not citing sources properly
- ❌ Skipping IRB requirements
- ❌ Missing consultation appointments
- ❌ Weak methods section (be specific!)
- ❌ Not proofreading final document
