**Date:** June 8-12, 2026

## Weekly Topic

Communicating technical research to non-technical audiences, including stakeholders, executives, and general public.

- **Due:** June 1, 2026
- **Points:** 100 points

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:
1. Adapt technical communication for different audiences
2. Simplify complex concepts without losing accuracy
3. Create executive summaries
4. Explain research value to stakeholders
5. Bridge technical and business communication

## Key Concepts

- Audience analysis
- Layered communication strategies
- Executive summaries
- Avoiding jargon
- Value proposition framing
- Impact communication
- Stakeholder engagement
