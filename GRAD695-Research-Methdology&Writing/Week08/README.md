## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week, students will be able to:
1. Understand principles of experimental design
2. Distinguish between experimental and quasi-experimental methods
3. Design controlled experiments
4. Identify confounding variables
5. Understand causal inference principles

## Key Concepts

- Randomized controlled trials (RCTs)
- Control groups and treatment groups
- Confounding variables and controls
- Quasi-experimental designs
- Internal and external validity
- A/B testing methodologies
- Causal inference frameworks

## Key Deliverables

- Experimental design for research question
- Identification of variables and controls
- Understanding of validity threats
