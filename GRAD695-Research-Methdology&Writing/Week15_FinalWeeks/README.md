## Final Proposal Requirements

### Format
- Double spaced, 12-pt font, 1-inch margins
- APA 7th edition style

### Required Sections
1. **Title Page** - Project title, student name, date
2. **Introduction** (1-2 pages) - Problem statement, objectives, significance
3. **Related Works/Literature Review** (3-5 pages) - Theoretical frameworks, prior research, gaps
4. **Methods** (2-4 pages) - Research design, data collection, analysis plan, validation
5. **References** - 20-30 citations in APA format

### Expected Length
- Total: 6-11 pages of content (plus title page and references)
- Quality over quantity - be concise and clear
